### Background

I spent 20 years in U.S. Army communications and information operations, including
assignments supporting Special Forces and Psychological Operations elements at Fort Bragg and
a deployment to Baghdad supporting a Joint Psychological Operations Task Force. Those
environments treated OPSEC and control of sensitive information as operational requirements
rather than compliance checkboxes.

As an Information Management Officer I validate system access requests before submission,
verifying the DD Form 2875 SAAR, training currency, and clearance status, and enforce the
approved system baseline across a fleet of user devices. That is where access control stopped
being a concept and became a daily process, and it shapes how I think about identity,
segmentation, and audit readiness in AWS.
